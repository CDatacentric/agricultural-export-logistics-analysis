## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural export and logistics transactions to evaluate the company's financial performance, operational efficiency, product and market performance, supplier contribution, and customer experience.

The project began with a dataset containing 5,051 records across 30 fields. I used Microsoft Excel and Power Query to structure, clean, validate, and transform the data before creating calculated analytical fields and using PivotTables to explore the dataset from multiple business perspectives.

The analysis culminated in an interactive executive dashboard that brings key financial and operational indicators together while allowing users to investigate performance by year, quarter, product, country, supplier, and shipping method.

The project also documents the analytical decisions made during data preparation, including the validation of financial calculations and the investigation and treatment of 152 missing Shipping Cost values.

---
## Business Problem

Management believes that valuable business opportunities and operational problems may be hidden within the company's transactional data. The objective of this analysis was to examine the business from both financial and operational perspectives, identify the factors contributing to overall performance, and uncover areas requiring further investigation.

The analysis focused on financial performance, product and market contribution, supplier performance, changes across time, logistics measures such as shipping cost, processing time and delivery delays, and customer satisfaction. The goal was to transform the available transaction data into reliable analysis, clear business findings, and evidence-based recommendations that could support better business decision-making.

# Key Business Questions Explored

The analysis was guided by the following business questions:

1. **How is the business performing financially in terms of revenue, net profit, and profit margin?**

2. **Which products, countries, and suppliers are driving revenue and profitability?**

3. **How has business performance changed across 2024 and 2025, including quarterly and monthly trends?**

4. **Where are the key operational opportunities and potential problems across shipping cost, processing time, and delivery delays?**

5. **What does the data reveal about customer satisfaction, and are there areas where financial performance and customer experience differ?**

6. **Which areas of the business require further investigation before management makes a decision?**

7. ## 📊 Data Source & Dataset Overview

### Data Source

This project uses a **synthetic agricultural export and logistics dataset generated for analytical practice and portfolio development**. It does not represent the actual operational records of a real company, and the company name used within the project is fictional.

The dataset was designed to simulate a realistic business environment containing transactional, financial, logistics, supplier, product, and customer-experience information. It provided an opportunity to practice the complete data analysis workflow while working with realistic data-quality challenges and business scenarios.

Because the dataset is synthetic, the findings should be interpreted as **analytical insights from the simulated dataset rather than verified statements about an actual company**.
